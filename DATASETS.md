# Data and model provenance

Primary sources checked 2026-09-27. Planned work is not reported as completed training.

| Asset | Actual use | Licence/source |
|---|---|---|
| COCO | Upstream YOLO11m pretraining; no team retraining or image redistribution | [COCO terms](https://cocodataset.org/#termsofuse): annotations/website CC BY 4.0; images remain individually owned and governed by Flickr terms. [Primary terms text](https://github.com/cocodataset/cocodataset.github.io/blob/master/dataset/termsofuse.htm). No blanket CC BY licence for every image |
| YOLO11m | Only shipped neural model | [Official docs](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo11/), [model card](https://huggingface.co/Ultralytics/YOLO11): AGPL-3.0 option; exact digest in weights/SHA256SUMS |
| Organizer C3905, C3896, C3897 | Development footage; C3905 perception/atlas and regression data | Contest rules in docs/task_spec.md. No separate public dataset licence supplied; redistribution permission outside contest use UNVERIFIED. Raw videos excluded from Git/releases |
| Camera geometry/atlas | Team-described scene and sample-derived statistical prior | Derived from organizer footage; not neural training. Flow frequency does not establish legality |
| Synthetic trajectories | Risk sanity checks and calibration configuration | Generated by tests/risk_fixtures.py, not real accident ground truth |
| Track-20 CSV fixture | Causal class regression | Derived observations documented in tests/fixtures/c3905_track20.md, not accident labels |

COCO attribution: COCO Consortium; Lin et al., *Microsoft COCO: Common Objects in Context*. Detector: Ultralytics. Tracking method: Zhang et al., *ByteTrack: Multi-Object Tracking by Associating Every Detection Box*.

No CADP, DoTA, CCD, DAD, UA-DETRAC, BDD100K, FASDD_CV or D-Fire data is used by the shipped pipeline. Mentions in plans do not establish licences or completed training. RT-DETR-L is a local challenger, not shipped. No learned hazard/near-miss/accident checkpoint is loaded.
